# Sample Proposal Acquisition Guide

This guide provides resources and strategies for acquiring realistic banking vendor proposals to test the TCO automation platform.

## Target Document Characteristics

### Primary Vendors (Core Banking Systems)
1. **FIS** - Look for HORIZON, Digital One, Payments One bundles
2. **Jack Henry** - Look for SilverLake, Xperience, Banno products
3. **CSI** - Core banking system proposals
4. **Fiserv** - DNA, Precision, Premier products
5. **Finastra** - Fusion banking products

### Document Structure to Target
- Pricing tables with monthly/annual/one-time fees
- Multi-year contract options (5/7/10 years)
- Service descriptions and deliverables
- Implementation timelines
- Executive summaries

### Complexity Levels
- **Simple** (10-20 pages): Basic service agreements
- **Medium** (30-50 pages): Standard vendor proposals
- **Complex** (60+ pages): Full RFP responses with appendices

## Public Sources for Sample Proposals

### 1. Government RFP Repositories

Government agencies often publish banking RFPs and vendor responses publicly:

**Municipal/County Banking Services RFPs:**
- Search: `"banking services RFP" site:gov filetype:pdf`
- Search: `"core banking" RFP response site:gov`
- Search: `"treasury management" proposal site:gov`

**Example Sources:**
- Decatur, GA Banking Services: https://www.decaturga.com (finance department)
- State/County government procurement portals
- City finance department websites

**How to Find:**
1. Go to any city/county website
2. Navigate to Finance or Procurement section
3. Look for "Bids & RFPs" or "Procurement"
4. Search for "banking" or "financial services"

### 2. Academic & Research Sources

**ResearchGate / Academia.edu:**
- Search: "core banking system implementation proposal"
- Search: "banking software RFP"
- Many implementation case studies include proposal excerpts

**University Research:**
- Banking technology research papers
- Fintech implementation studies

### 3. Template & Sample Sites

**Proposal Templates (for structure reference):**
- ProposalKit - Banking services templates
- Proposable - Financial services proposals
- PandaDoc - Banking proposal templates
- Better Proposals - Finance templates

**Note:** Templates show structure but may lack realistic pricing data.

### 4. Industry Publications

**Banking Technology Publications:**
- American Banker
- Bank Technology News
- Credit Union Times
- Search archives for vendor comparison articles

### 5. Public Records Requests

Many states have open records laws allowing access to RFP responses:
- File FOIA/FOIL requests for banking services RFP responses
- Usually available from state/county procurement offices

## What to Look For in Documents

### Must-Have Elements
- [ ] Pricing tables (monthly fees, one-time fees)
- [ ] Service/product names matching vendor terminology
- [ ] Contract term options
- [ ] Implementation costs

### Nice-to-Have Elements
- [ ] Volume-based pricing
- [ ] Annual increase rates (CPI)
- [ ] Optional services section
- [ ] Third-party integrations
- [ ] Support/maintenance fees

### Red Flags (Documents to Avoid)
- Marketing brochures without pricing
- Heavily redacted documents
- Non-US banking proposals (different terminology)
- Documents older than 2015 (outdated pricing models)

## File Naming Convention

When saving acquired proposals, use this naming convention:

```
[vendor-type]_[source]_[complexity]_[sequence].pdf

Examples:
- fis_gov_rfp_simple_001.pdf
- jh_research_medium_001.xlsx
- csi_template_complex_001.pdf
- fiserv_municipal_medium_001.pdf
```

Place files in the appropriate subdirectory:
- `dummy-proposals/fis-like/` - FIS-style documents
- `dummy-proposals/jack-henry-like/` - Jack Henry-style documents
- `dummy-proposals/csi-like/` - CSI-style documents
- `dummy-proposals/other-vendors/` - Other banking vendors

## Target Collection Goals

### Minimum (5-7 documents):
- 2 FIS-like proposals
- 2 Jack Henry-like proposals
- 1 CSI-like proposal
- 1-2 other vendor proposals

### Recommended (10-12 documents):
- 3-4 FIS-like (varying complexity)
- 3-4 Jack Henry-like (varying complexity)
- 2 CSI-like
- 2-3 other vendors (Fiserv, Finastra, etc.)

### Maximum (15 documents):
- Even distribution across vendor types
- Mix of complexity levels
- Various document formats (PDF, DOCX, XLSX)

---

## Legal Considerations

- Only use publicly available documents
- Respect terms of service on download sites
- Do not use confidential/proprietary proposals
- Redact any sensitive information (names, specific bank details)
- Use documents only for testing purposes
